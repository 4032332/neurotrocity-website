Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We don't know who you are.
  • The deals, goals and screenshots you save stay on your device and sync only through your own private iCloud. We can't see them.
  • DisPoint never links to a loyalty account and never stores a loyalty password.
  • The free app shows non-personalised ads through Google AdMob. Pro removes them.
  • This website uses Google Analytics; the app contains no analytics SDK.

DisPoint is a personal tool for saving discount codes and loyalty-points deals you find yourself, with reminders before they expire. This policy explains what data the app and this website handle, and how. It applies to the DisPoint iOS app and the pages under neurotrocity.com/dispoint, published by NeuroTrocity.

1. Information you create in the app

  • The discount codes, points deals, goals, reminder settings, and any notes or screenshots you add are stored on your device using Apple's on-device database.
  • If you are signed into iCloud, this data syncs across your own Apple devices through Apple's CloudKit (your private iCloud). It is not sent to us and we cannot access it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • DisPoint does not ask for or store any login or credentials for loyalty programs (Qantas, Velocity, Flybuys, Everyday Rewards, or any other). It never connects to your loyalty accounts. This is a permanent product boundary.
  • You can optionally type a display name into the app. That name is stored only on your device.

2. No account, and no analytics in the app

  • DisPoint has no sign-up or login and collects no name, email, or contact details in order to run.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or usage-tracking SDKs.

3. Advertising

DisPoint's free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are served non-personalised — the app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use your advertising identifier to track you across other apps or websites. To serve and measure ads, Google's ad SDK may process standard technical signals such as a device identifier, coarse (non-precise) location, and ad-interaction data, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's AdMob policies. Purchasing DisPoint Pro removes all ads.

4. Following creators, reporting and blocking

DisPoint lets you follow invite-only creators whose deal posts appear on your board. Creator posts are public content published to the app — following one does not share anything from your own library with the creator or with us, and your saved items are never uploaded.

If you report a piece of creator content, the report — including the item you reported and any message you write — is sent to us so we can review it. We use it only to moderate content. If you block a creator, that choice is stored on your device.

5. Other network requests from the app

  • Deal catalogue. The app downloads a public, curated list of Australian promos. This is a one-way download — no information about you is sent.
  • Retailer logos. To show a shop's logo, the app may request a small icon from a public favicon service using the retailer's website domain (for example coles.com.au). No personal information is included in these requests.

6. Purchases

DisPoint Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. DisPoint does not receive or store your payment details. Purchase handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, DisPoint schedules local reminders on your device before a code or deal expires. These are generated on-device; no push server is used.

8. This website

Unlike the app, neurotrocity.com/dispoint uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages and links they use. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes technical information such as your IP address (truncated by Google), device and browser type, approximate location, referring site, and the pages you view. We use this only in aggregate, to measure whether our advertising is working. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything from the app.

You can opt out with Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, by blocking cookies in your browser, or by enabling your browser's tracking protection. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If you send us a message through the contact form, your name, email address and message are transmitted through our form provider and delivered to our support inbox. We use them only to reply to you.

9. Children

DisPoint is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Your choices

  • Delete any saved item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes its on-device data from that device.
  • Manage ad and tracking settings in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Manage iCloud sync under Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Opt out of this website's analytics using any of the methods in section 8.

11. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or make a complaint. Because the app holds no personal information on our side, such a request will usually relate only to a support email, a content report, or website analytics. Email us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: dispoint@neurotrocity.com.
