      How it's made

      Cut to your order,
      not to a warehouse.

      We hold timber, not finished furniture. Your uprights are docked the week you order, which is why it takes three to five weeks and why the grain matches.

      Week one

      Docked and dressed

      Boards are selected for your order and machined to size. You get an email with a photograph of the actual stack.

      Week two

      Channels routed

      Every upright is routed on the same jig, which is what makes a 610 bought in 2016 fit one bought this year.

      Week three

      Oiled and cured

      Three coats of hard-wax oil, cut back between each. It cures for four days before it can be wrapped.

      Week four

      Flat-packed

      Wrapped in wool felt and cardboard, no plastic. It arrives as a bundle of parts and an A5 card.
