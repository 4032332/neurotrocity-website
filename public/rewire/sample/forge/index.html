    The room

    A warehouse
    with good iron in it.

    Six hundred square metres on Maitland Road. Twelve platforms, two turf lanes, and no mirrors anywhere. There is no juice bar and there never will be.

    What it costs

    Three prices.
    No contract.

    Weekly, paid fortnightly, cancel with two weeks' notice. No joining fee, no minimum term, and no twelve-month direct debit you have to ring a call centre to escape.

    Starting

    Your first month.

    Most people who join have either never lifted or haven't in years. Nobody is thrown into a session on day one.

    Week 0

    Free intro, 45 minutes

    One-on-one with Mara. What you want, what hurts, what you've done before. You lift nothing heavier than a broomstick.

    Week 1

    Two beginner sessions

    Capped at four instead of eight. Squat, hinge, press, pull. You will be sore and it will be manageable.

    Week 2

    Into normal sessions

    You join the general timetable with your own numbers on the board. The coach still watches every set.

    Week 4

    First re-test

    We measure the same things we measured in week one and show you the difference, whether or not it flatters us.
