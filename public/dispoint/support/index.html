Common questions

Do I need an account?

No — DisPoint has no sign-up. Your saved deals stay on your device and sync through your own private iCloud.

Does it connect to my Qantas, Velocity, Flybuys or Everyday Rewards account?

No. DisPoint never links to loyalty accounts and never stores their logins. You update balances yourself. This is a permanent product boundary, not a missing feature.

How do reminders work?

DisPoint schedules local notifications before a code or deal expires — by default 7 days out, 1 day out, and the morning of. With Pro you can override the timing on any individual item. The calendar view shows every upcoming expiry in a month at a glance.

My deals aren't syncing to my other device.

Sync uses your own iCloud, so check that both devices are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on under Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Sync can take a minute on first launch, and needs a network connection on both devices.

The screenshot capture read the wrong details.

Text recognition is best-effort and every field is editable before you save — just correct it on the confirmation form. If a field looked uncertain, DisPoint leaves it blank rather than guessing.

Are the points figures guaranteed?

No. The Point Potential calculator gives an estimate based on what you enter and the assumptions printed on the screen, including the fixed 2:1 conversions (Everyday Rewards to Qantas, Flybuys to Velocity). Loyalty programs change their rules — always confirm with the retailer or program before relying on a figure.

What does DisPoint Pro include?

A one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited goals, all ten themes and alternate app icons, CSV export, per-item reminder timing, and the advanced calculator.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open the app's settings and use Restore purchases, making sure you're signed into the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

How do I get my data out?

Pro includes CSV export of your library from Settings.

What does DisPoint need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. DisPoint is built for Australia — the retailers, programs and conversion rules assume an Australian user.
