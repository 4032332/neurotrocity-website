Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your library lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud, so it's already on your other devices without you doing anything.
Does it connect to my Qantas, Velocity, Flybuys or Everyday Rewards account?
No — and it never will. Those programs expose no public way to read a member's balance, and using a member's login on their behalf is against their terms. You update balances yourself. This is a permanent product boundary, not a feature we haven't got to yet.
Is DisPoint a deal feed like Cashrewards or ShopBack?
Not quite. It's primarily a private library of the codes and runs you found. You can also follow invite-only creators whose drops land on your board, but nothing is shared from your library to anyone else.
How accurate is the points calculator?
It's an estimate and says so on screen. It multiplies your planned spend by the deal's rate and your program's earn rate, then applies the fixed 2:1 conversions (Everyday Rewards to Qantas, Flybuys to Velocity). Programs change their rules, so always confirm with the retailer or program before relying on a figure.
What does it cost?
Nothing. DisPoint is free, with every feature available to everyone — there's no subscription and nothing to unlock. A small banner ad on the Home screen and the For You board pays for it.
Which countries is it for?
DisPoint is built for Australia — the retailers, loyalty programs and conversion rules all assume an Australian user. It'll run anywhere, but the program pairings won't mean much outside AU.
What does it need?
An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
