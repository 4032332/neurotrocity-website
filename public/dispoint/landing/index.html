Capture

You already screenshot the deal. That's the whole workflow.

Share any screenshot to DisPoint and on-device text recognition reads the retailer, the offer, the code and the expiry. You get a pre-filled form to check — and anything it isn't sure about is left blank rather than guessed, because a wrong pre-fill costs more than an empty field.

  • Works from Photos, Safari, Mail, Messages — anywhere with a share sheet
  • Recognition runs entirely on your device; no server of ours sees it
  • The original screenshot stays attached to the item

Point Potential

What's "20x points" actually worth to you?

Enter what you plan to spend and DisPoint turns the headline multiplier into a real figure — then applies the fixed conversion to the frequent flyer program those points actually land in. Everyday Rewards pairs to Qantas, Flybuys pairs to Velocity, both at 2:1. You never have to pick the pairing; it's derived, not guessed.

  • Store-rewards deals show the frequent-flyer equivalent alongside
  • The assumption is printed on screen, permanently — never buried in a tooltip
  • Mark a deal as acquired and the points post straight to a goal

Figures are estimates, not a promise of what you'll earn.

Goals

See the whole trip, not just a number.

Set a goal — "Japan 2027, 200,000 Qantas points" — add the route, and DisPoint breaks it into milestones for each leg, including the flight home, plus accommodation. They tick off as your balance grows, and a little plane travels the runway as you go.

  • Trip, Product, Point Total or Other — goals aren't flights-only
  • Searchable airport directory for building a route
  • A projected completion date, clearly labelled an estimate
  • Every change is an append-only entry, so the history stays honest

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your library lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud, so it's already on your other devices without you doing anything.
Does it connect to my Qantas, Velocity, Flybuys or Everyday Rewards account?
No — and it never will. Those programs expose no public way to read a member's balance, and using a member's login on their behalf is against their terms. You update balances yourself. This is a permanent product boundary, not a feature we haven't got to yet.
Is DisPoint a deal feed like Cashrewards or ShopBack?
Not quite. It's primarily a private library of the codes and runs you found. You can also follow invite-only creators whose drops land on your board, but nothing is shared from your library to anyone else.
How accurate is the points calculator?
It's an estimate and says so on screen. It multiplies your planned spend by the deal's rate and your program's earn rate, then applies the fixed 2:1 conversions (Everyday Rewards to Qantas, Flybuys to Velocity). Programs change their rules, so always confirm with the retailer or program before relying on a figure.
What does Pro cost?
Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. The current price shows in the app and on the App Store listing for your region.
Which countries is it for?
DisPoint is built for Australia — the retailers, loyalty programs and conversion rules all assume an Australian user. It'll run anywhere, but the program pairings won't mean much outside AU.
What does it need?
An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
