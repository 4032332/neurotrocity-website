Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. DoseTrack works fully without one, and your data stays on your device. An account is only needed if you want cross-device sync or to manage a loved one's medications.
Will the reminders actually go off?
That's the thing the app is built around. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and fire on both iPhone and Apple Watch, so they don't depend on a server being up or a network connection.
Will my medications show on my lock screen?
Never the dose or pill count. And if you'd rather not see medication names either, you can hide them from notifications entirely in Settings.
What happens after 5 medications?
The first five are free forever, with no time limit and no feature stripped out. Adding a sixth needs DoseTrack Plus, which starts with a 7-day free trial.
Can I get my data out?
Yes, at any time. CSV export is free. Plus adds a formatted PDF adherence report designed to hand to a doctor.
Can I manage medications for a parent or partner?
Yes — caregiver sharing is part of DoseTrack Plus, and lets you manage a loved one's medications remotely.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. DoseTrack is currently iPhone and Apple Watch only, and needs iOS 17 or later.
DoseTrack is not a medical device. It helps you remember and record doses; it does not provide medical advice, and it should not be used as your only safeguard for a critical medication. Always follow the directions from your doctor or pharmacist. Read the full disclaimer →
