Every corner is the real corner.

This road is not drawn. Its 700 points come from OpenStreetMap and its height from the ASTER global elevation model. When it climbs, the climb happened.

The blue line is the actual coast.

Not a suggestion of one. It is the Southern Ocean shoreline as surveyed, hatched seaward, sitting on the datum beneath the road so you can see how close the two run.

Apollo Bay, then up.

The road leaves the water and climbs into the Otways. Every orange hair is a gradient reading; they thicken because the climb does.
