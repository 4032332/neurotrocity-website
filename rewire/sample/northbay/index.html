Your first session

What the hour actually looks like.

00 minutes in 1 of 5
00 — 12 min

You talk, we listen

What happened, what makes it worse, what you've already tried, and what you're actually trying to get back to. Nobody types into a computer with their back to you.

12 — 25 min

Assessment

Movement, strength, and the specific tests that separate the three or four things it's likely to be. You'll be told what we're ruling out, and why.

25 — 30 min

The explanation

In plain English, with the diagram if it helps. If we don't know yet, you'll be told that too, along with what would tell us.

30 — 50 min

Hands-on treatment

Twenty minutes of actual manual therapy: soft tissue, joint mobilisation, dry needling if it's indicated and you're comfortable with it.

50 — 60 min

Two or three things to do

Not twelve. Two or three exercises you'll actually remember, sent to your phone before you've reached the car park, plus what to expect over the next week.

Why we're different

The twenty-minuteappointment is abusiness decision.

It isn't a clinical one. Booking three patients an hour across two rooms earns a clinic roughly double what we earn, and it's why so many people have been through four sessions of the same ultrasound and left none the wiser.

We charge more per visit and see fewer people. In return you get an hour of somebody's actual attention, and most patients need fewer visits because of it. That's the whole model. There isn't a catch.

  • Sixty minutes, every appointment

    Initial and follow-up. The clock doesn't change because you're a return patient.

  • Hands on, every session

    If a session is only exercise prescription, we'll tell you beforehand so you can decide whether it's worth the trip.

  • The same physio, every time

    Northbay is a single-practitioner practice. You will not be reassigned.
